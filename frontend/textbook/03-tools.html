Tools & Order Types

The instruments and order mechanics traders rely on — and where to find their equivalents in QuantSphere.

Order types

Market order
Buy or sell immediately at the current live price. Fast, but you accept whatever the price is at that instant (subject to slippage). This is what QuantSphere's "Buy at Market" / "Sell at Market" buttons do.
Limit order
An order to buy below the current price or sell above it — used when you want a better entry than what's available right now, and are willing to wait for price to come to you.
Stop order
An order to buy above the current price or sell below it — commonly used to enter on a breakout, once price proves it's actually moving in that direction.
Stop-loss / Take-profit orders
Attached to an open position to automatically close it at a predefined loss or gain — the backbone of hands-off risk management.

Charting tools you'll see everywhere

  • Moving Average (MA) — smooths price into a single line by averaging the last N periods. A simple way to gauge trend direction; price above a rising MA often signals an uptrend.
  • RSI (Relative Strength Index) — a momentum oscillator from 0–100. Above 70 is generally considered "overbought," below 30 "oversold." QuantSphere's Pre-Trade Check reads this automatically from your live price history.
  • MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) — tracks the relationship between two moving averages to highlight momentum shifts and potential trend changes.
  • Bollinger Bands — a moving average with bands plotted above/below based on volatility; price hugging or piercing a band can signal an extended move.
  • Volume — how much was traded in a period. Rising price on rising volume is a healthier signal than the same move on thin volume.
